Request for Proposal (RFP)

Sales Partnership Briefing

Framework & requirements for cold acquisition and sales partners (dobetr.de)

Product & Market Overview

  • Product: PACE Test (Performance And Competition Evaluation – A scientifically validated and reliable online assessment for athletes' mental skills.
  • Target Audience: Exclusively sports clubs, coaches, sports federations/associations, and sports psychologists. (No corporate HR/B2B).
  • Language: English only.
  • Pricing Model: €1,200 annual license per team, coach, or sports psychologist.

Status Quo & Assets

  • The Challenge: Lack of internal manpower and an established, scalable cold outreach process.
  • Available Assets:
    Website
    dobetr.de
    Socials
    Insta & LinkedIn
    Outreach
    CRM Setup

Scope of Work

We are not seeking high-ticket sales coaching. We require hands-on execution or a ready-to-use setup:

  • Model A: Done-For-You (SDR / Outreach Agency): Full execution of cold outreach (Email campaigns, Instagram DMs) to generate qualified demo calls or direct license sales.
  • Model B: Done-With-You (Project-Based Setup): Creation of ready-to-use outreach scripts, sales funnels, and proven sequences tailored specifically to the international sports sector.

Proposal Requirements (Your Pitch)

Please submit a concise proposal covering the following four areas:

  1. Strategic Approach: Recommended acquisition channels for reaching English-speaking sports coaches, federations, and psychologists.
  2. Target KPIs: Projected numbers of qualified contacts, demo calls, or sales on a weekly/monthly basis.
  3. Compensation Structure: Monthly retainer / base fee (if applicable) + Performance commission (e.g. per demo or closed license).
  4. Timeline & Onboarding: Estimated timeframe from agreement to launching the first campaign.
Important Commercial Note: With an annual license value of €1,200, maintaining a healthy, sustainable Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) to revenue ratio and a fair risk-sharing model is critical to us.
